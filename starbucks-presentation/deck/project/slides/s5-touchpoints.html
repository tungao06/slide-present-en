The digital Third Place

Slide 5 of 8 · Part 3 · Operationalizing Experience

How do you keep a human experience intact across thousands of stores? Through everyday touchpoints. First, personalization and connection: names on cups, eye contact from the barista, and drinks customized by hand. Second, sensory consistency: the same ambient design, signature scents and curated playlists in stores around the world. Third, community customization: store architecture that reflects the neighborhood's heritage while the core brand values stay the same. As customer lifestyles changed, Starbucks had to bring the Third Place into the digital age.
