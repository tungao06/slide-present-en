Human experience, at scale

Slide 4 of 8 · Part 2 · Concept Development

Schultz's answer was the Third Place. Home is the first place, work is the second, and Starbucks becomes the third: a comforting, pressure-free sanctuary between the two. The second pillar is emotional connection, captured in Schultz's line: we aren't in the coffee business serving people; we're in the people business serving coffee. The third pillar treats the atmosphere itself as a product: warm lighting, wooden textures, acoustic music and a welcoming aroma.
