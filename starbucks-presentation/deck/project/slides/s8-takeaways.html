Slide 8 of 8 · Part 4 · MBA Lessons

Three lessons for business leaders. First, product versus experience: products can be commoditized, but a brand experience builds a competitive moat. Second, authenticity and consistency: core brand values must stay the same even during rapid global expansion. Third, strategic adaptability: Starbucks merged the physical third-place atmosphere with a cutting-edge digital ecosystem. We close with this thought: in a world that is increasingly digital and isolated, the hunger for authentic human connection has never been greater.
