Slide 2 of 8 · Part 1 · The Foundation & Origin

Before the lattes, Starbucks was a high-end bean and spice retailer. It was founded by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl and Gordon Bowker, and its mission was to teach Americans how to brew fine dark-roast coffee. The logo came from a 16th-century Norse woodcut of a twin-tailed siren, a nod to Seattle's nautical tradition and to the irresistible pull of fine coffee. The stage was set, but Starbucks was still missing its biggest magic ingredient, until 1983 and a trip to Milan.
