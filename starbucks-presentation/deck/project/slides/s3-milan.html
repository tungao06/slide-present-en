Slide 3 of 8 · Part 2 · The Turning Point

In 1983 Howard Schultz visited the espresso bars of Milan. He noticed that they were not just selling drinks; they worked as vibrant community living rooms. His vision was that America needed an authentic espresso culture built around human connection, speed and craftsmanship. In 1987 he bought Starbucks for 3.8 million dollars and merged his Il Giornale cafe concept into it, launching the modern coffeehouse era. Schultz didn't just want to sell coffee; he wanted to create a new space in human life.
