Today the story is told in more than 38,000 stores across more than 80 markets. Three moves made that scale possible. Strategic joint ventures with local partners opened new markets. Market customization adapted the menu, for example Teavana and matcha drinks in Asia. And the high-end Reserve Roasteries act as flagship storytellers for the whole brand.
