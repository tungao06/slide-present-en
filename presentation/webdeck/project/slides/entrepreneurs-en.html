Real small businesses

Human judgment

Article 5 moves from consultants to entrepreneurs in Kenya. Half of the small-business owners received a GPT-4-powered business assistant that gave advice; the outcomes were revenue and profit. On average, the researchers could not reject the null hypothesis of no treatment effect. But the average hides two stories. Entrepreneurs who were low performers at baseline did nearly 10% worse with the assistant, while high performers may have benefited by more than 15%. The questions and the AI advice were similar; the difference lay in which advice the entrepreneurs chose to implement. I want to be careful here: these are subgroup averages, not a rule for every individual.
